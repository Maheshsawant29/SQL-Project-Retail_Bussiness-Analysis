# 🏪 Retail Optical Store Business Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a complete end-to-end Business Analytics project built using SQL on a simulated Retail Optical Store database.

The objective of this project is to transform raw transactional business data into actionable business insights that can help management make informed decisions regarding customer retention, sales growth, staff performance, referral marketing, product optimization, and overall business profitability.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow including database design, SQL data analysis, dashboard development, and business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project aims to answer key business questions such as:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which age groups generate the highest revenue?
- How effectively are customers converted into repeat buyers?
- Which customer segments contribute the highest lifetime value?
- How effective are referral customers?
- What factors contribute to customer churn?
- Which staff members perform most consistently?
- Which products and brands generate maximum revenue?
- What strategic actions can improve long-term profitability?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

Some major insights discovered during the analysis include:

- Senior Customers (60+) are the largest revenue contributors.
- Repeat Customers represent only 28% of buyers but contribute nearly 47% of total revenue.
- Referral Customers generate approximately 10% of total revenue without marketing expenditure.
- First-Time Buyer Churn (51.69%) is significantly higher than Repeat Customer Churn (22.36%).
- Glasses contribute the highest product revenue (₹1.48 Crore).
- Progressive Lenses provide the highest-margin business opportunity.
- Staff performance remains highly consistent across all KPIs.
- Product revenue is well diversified across multiple brands, reducing supplier dependency.
